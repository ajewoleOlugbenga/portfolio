How I write

Good copy starts with a clear purpose.

The tone moves from educational to aspirational to direct-response. The process stays the same.

  1. Start with the objectiveDefine what the reader should know, feel or do.
  2. Earn attentionUse a relevant hook, not noise for its own sake.
  3. Make the value clearKeep it specific, relevant and easy to follow.
  4. Match the voiceWrite in a tone that fits the brand and its audience.
  5. Give a next stepClose with a clear, purposeful call to action.
